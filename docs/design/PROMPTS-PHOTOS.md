# Prompts de génération des photos de remplacement (Q7)

Phase 3 V3, 20 nov. 2026. Ces prompts reprennent les briefs de `docs/design/REFERENCES.md` § 2. Félix les a demandés le 20 nov. Ils sont rédigés en anglais, la langue qui donne le rendu photographique le plus précis dans les générateurs d'images. Chaque image produite reste une **illustration** : l'interface la marque « Illustration », et elle n'est jamais montrée comme la photo d'un spot réel.

**Conseils d'usage**
- Générer 3 ou 4 variantes par prompt, puis garder celle dont l'anatomie du poisson et les mains sont exactes.
- Envoyer le fichier original à la plus haute résolution : 1 600 px au moins sur le grand côté, 2 000 px ou plus si possible.
- Nommer les fichiers comme ceci :
    - `bar-eau-claire.jpg` ;
    - `lieu-noir-pont-jig.jpg` ;
    - `platier-aube-frontale.jpg` ;
    - `jigging-console-sondeur.jpg`.

**Contrôle avant envoi**
- Le poisson est le bon : il n'a pas de rayures, ses nageoires et sa ligne latérale sont correctes.
- Aucun visage reconnaissable, aucun texte, logo, marque ni filigrane.
- Les mains ont cinq doigts.
- Aucun lieu identifiable : pas de phare, de port ni de côte reconnaissable.
- Le ratio est respecté, et la zone calme prévue pour le texte de l'interface est libre.

## 1. Bar tenu au-dessus de l'eau claire (remplace `bar-boudin`)

Usage : carte d'espèce « bar » et illustration BORD ; 3:4 portrait.

```text
Photorealistic editorial outdoor photograph, full-frame camera, 50 mm lens at f/2.8, natural light only.

SUBJECT: a freshly caught European sea bass (Dicentrarchus labrax), about 55 cm long, held horizontally by an angler 20–30 cm above crystal-clear shallow seawater, just before release. The angler's wet bare hands support it correctly: one hand under the belly just behind the head, the other near the tail. The fish is anatomically exact: elongated silver body, darker blue-grey back, bright white belly, NO stripes at all, two clearly separate dorsal fins (the first one spiny and raised), a dark spot on the upper edge of the gill cover, large mouth, crisp scales covered with water droplets.

LURE: a natural-coloured soft plastic paddle-tail shad (about 12 cm) on a lead jig head, still hooked in the corner of the mouth, the braided line leading out of frame.

SETTING: shore fishing from the rocks, not from a boat. The angler kneels on dark granite at the water's edge. Under the fish the water is so clear that pale sand, pebbles and strands of brown kelp are visible on the bottom. No boat, no inflatable tube, no pier.

LIGHT: low raking golden sun from the side, about 20 minutes after sunrise: rim light along the fish's back, sparkles on the water, cool blue shadows. Calm sea, light breeze.

FRAMING: vertical 3:4, close-medium shot. The fish fills the middle third of the frame, slightly above centre. Only the hands, the forearms (dark technical jacket sleeves) and the lower chest of the angler are visible; the face is out of frame. The bottom quarter of the image is calm clear water with little detail (space for interface text). Shallow depth of field; the background is a soft blur of rocks and sea.

STYLE: authentic premium fishing-magazine photograph. Natural, slightly desaturated Atlantic colours (navy, slate grey, sand, kelp brown); the warm sunlight is the only warm accent. No teal-and-orange grading, no HDR, no CGI look.

AVOID: striped bass, any stripes on the fish, blood or a dead-looking fish, gloves, a fish hanging vertically by the jaw, any boat or inflatable tube, lighthouses, harbours or recognisable coastline, faces, text, logos, brand names on the gear, watermarks, extra or deformed fingers.
```

## 2. Lieu noir sur le pont, jig accroché (remplace `lieu-noir-boudin`)

Usage : carte d'espèce « lieu noir », mode BATEAU ; 3:4 portrait.

```text
Photorealistic documentary photograph, 35 mm lens at f/4, soft overcast daylight.

SUBJECT: a freshly caught saithe / coalfish (Pollachius virens), about 65 cm, lying on its side on the wet deck of an offshore fishing boat, shown in full side view from slightly above, head to the left. Anatomically exact: very dark olive-green to almost black back fading to silvery-grey flanks and a pale belly; a STRAIGHT, clearly PALE (whitish) lateral line, not the curved dark lateral line of a pollack; three separate dorsal fins and two anal fins; forked tail; lower jaw slightly protruding; large dark eye.

LURE: a slim metallic vertical jig (about 18 cm, 200 g, plain silver-blue holographic finish, no brand) still attached by its assist hook in the corner of the jaw, with a short length of braided line and a clear fluorocarbon leader curling on the deck.

SETTING: offshore, on the deck of a small centre-console boat. Textured grey non-slip deck with seawater puddles. At the edge of the frame: part of a rod butt and the strap of a yellow lifejacket, or a coiled rope. No inflatable tube visible. Beyond the gunwale, a strip of dark grey-blue open sea with a long swell, no land.

LIGHT: soft, even, cool overcast light with specular highlights on the wet skin; moody Atlantic atmosphere.

FRAMING: vertical 3:4. The fish lies diagonally across the frame, from lower left to upper right, and spans about 70 % of the width. The top quarter is calm deck and sea texture with little detail (space for interface text). Sharp from head to tail.

STYLE: authentic premium editorial photograph, natural slightly desaturated colours, rich detail on scales and fins. No teal-and-orange grading, no HDR, no CGI look.

AVOID: a person holding the fish, the inflatable tube of a RIB, pollack features (curved dark lateral line, bronze colour), cod features (spots, chin barbel), blood, gore, brand names or logos, text, watermark, extra fins, deformed anatomy.
```

## 3. Platier découvert avant l'aube, frontale rouge (remplace `silhouette-aube-rose`)

Usage : illustration BORD (écrans généraux, états vides) ; 3:4 portrait.

```text
Photorealistic landscape photograph, 24 mm lens on a tripod, blue-hour exposure.

SUBJECT: a lone lure angler walking carefully across a large rocky reef flat uncovered at low tide, about 30 minutes before sunrise. He wears a headlamp set to RED light (a small red beam visible on the rocks just ahead of him), chest waders, a dark jacket and a small chest bag, and carries a spinning rod pointed forward. He is small in the frame (about one sixth of the image height), seen from three-quarter behind; his face is not visible.

SETTING: a wide intertidal flat of dark rock covered with brown wrack seaweed, scattered tide pools mirroring the sky, the low-tide waterline with gentle small waves in the middle distance, open sea to the horizon. A generic Atlantic coast (Brittany or Normandy feel) with NO identifiable landmark: no lighthouse, no buildings, no distinctive island or rock shape.

LIGHT AND SKY: deep blue hour. A navy-blue sky fading to a thin band of pale warm light on the horizon, a few last stars. No pink or purple sky, no sun disc. The only strong warm accent is the red glow of the headlamp. Calm, cold, quiet atmosphere.

FRAMING: vertical 3:4. Horizon in the upper third; the angler on the right third, walking towards the sea; foreground tide pools leading the eye to him. The bottom third is dark, low-detail rock and seaweed (space for interface text).

STYLE: realistic, subtle, premium outdoor photography with natural night tones, clean and noise-free. No HDR, no fantasy glow.

AVOID: pink or purple dawn sky, a sun disc, a silhouette standing on a single rock against a coloured sky, lighthouses, harbours, faces, a white headlamp beam, text, logos, watermark.
```

## 4. Jigging vertical vu de la console, sondeur à 60–80 m (remplace `semi-rigide-gilets`)

Usage : illustration du mode BATEAU (en-tête, écran d'entrée) ; 16:9 paysage.

```text
Photorealistic action photograph, 24 mm lens at f/5.6, taken from just behind the centre console of a 6–7 m rigid-inflatable boat, offshore.

FOREGROUND (right third, the edge slightly out of focus): the console with a modern colour fish finder showing a clean echogram: a hard seabed line at about 70 m depth and a cloud of fish echoes just above the bottom. Any number on the screen must be sharp, plausible and consistent (depth between 68 and 72 m); if numbers cannot be rendered cleanly, show the echogram with no numbers at all.

MAIN SUBJECT (centre-left, in focus): one angler standing at the side of the boat, wearing a clearly visible inflatable lifejacket and a cap, vertical jigging: the rod is bent hard over the side and the braided line goes perfectly vertical into deep dark-blue water. Three-quarter back view; the face is not visible.

SETTING: open sea with a long low swell; no land, or only a faint generic coastline on the horizon. Grey overcast sky with a slight brightening.

LIGHT: soft diffuse daylight, cool tones, wet surfaces.

FRAMING: horizontal 16:9. The angler and the vertical line fill the left two-thirds, the screen the right third. The top fifth of the image is plain sky (space for interface text).

STYLE: authentic premium fishing-magazine photograph; natural desaturated Atlantic colours (navy, slate, grey); the orange of the lifejacket is the only warm accent. No teal-and-orange grading, no HDR, no CGI look.

AVOID: top-down or drone views of the boat, several people posing, anyone without a lifejacket, bright tropical water, brand names or logos on the boat, the engine or the screen, garbled text, watermark.
```
